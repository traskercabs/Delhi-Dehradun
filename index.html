The Delhi to Dehradun Route

The journey from Delhi NCR to Dehradun covers approximately 250km. Depending on highway traffic and the newly developing Delhi-Dehradun Expressway sections (including the Saharanpur bypass), you can expect a realistic travel time of 3 to 4 hours. For the smoothest highway exit, we highly recommend scheduling an early morning departure.

Airport & City Pickups: Our service includes doorstep pickups anywhere across Delhi NCR, including direct terminal pickups from IGI Airport (T1, T2, T3). Upon arrival, we offer direct drop-offs to your chosen destination in Dehradun, including popular areas like ISBT, Rajpur Road, Clement Town, and local city hotels—wherever your journey takes you.
